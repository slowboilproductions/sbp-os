## Doer's read (design-partner notes — for the operator to confirm/redirect)
- **This is a THIRD flavor of dark, distinct from the two he already walked from.** Not Control Room (devtool/crypto dark) and not After Hours (backbar/luxury dark) — this is **machine-shop / precision-industrial** dark. It aligns strongly with the *actual* SBP brand voice (no Madison-Avenue suits · a working shop not a lounge · precision · "built for our world, by someone in it" · heat under the surface). So it is not a repeat of a rejected thing; it is a different register that happens to fit the voice.
- **The real tension to hold (surface, don't smooth):** the palette's stated world is *automotive/construction/heavy industry*; SBP sells to *developing spirits / bev-alc brand owners*. Black+grey+red = power+precision reads universally, so the palette ports — but literal heavy-industry motifs (rivets, hazard stripes, blueprint grids) would drift off-category AND trip the taste skill's decorative-grid/stripe bans. **Adopt the palette's power/precision essence; execute it in SBP's own world** (bottles, the label press, depletion data, back-of-house) — mirror-never-mint applied to a mood board.
- **Screenshots-on-dark (launch item 1) — solved by the 4th swatch.** Dark grounds are the hardest home for product screenshots, but **Light Concrete `#DFE6E9`** gives a real light surface. A deliberate two-surface system — dark for brand/narrative/hero, Light-Concrete panels for the product showcases (CRM, Operational Dashboard) — keeps screenshots legible while staying on-system. Must be a *documented device*, not random section inversion (skill's theme-lock rule).
- **Accent discipline is already right:** one accent (Racing Red), used for live states + CTAs only. Contrast gets verified at build (white bold label on `#D63031` is fine for large/button text; small red text on obsidian needs checking — likely restricted to large/accents).
