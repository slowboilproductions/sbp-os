## The direction (operator's words, 2026-07-05)
> "A classic black and white (not true black or true white) base with **red and gray as the accent**."

Refines the `palette-card.png` seed ("Industrial Urban" card). **Operator correction, recorded:** the card's automotive/construction/heavy-industry framing is rejected — *"industry don't own colors."* Drop the industrial motif thinking entirely; keep the colors. The direction is **classic B&W precision**, not a machine-shop costume.

The shift that matters: this is a **two-ground system where off-white is co-equal**, not dark-dominant. That is what makes B&W + one hot red age well AND gives product screenshots (launch item 1) a natural light home — the dark-vs-screenshots worry dissolves.

## Two-surface system (the load-bearing idea)
- **Light sections** (off-white ground): brand-neutral, product showcases, data panels — where CRM / Operational Dashboard screenshots live and stay legible.
- **Dark sections** (off-black ground): the brand-narrative / heat-under-the-surface moments (the wall, the promise) — where dark earns its place.
- Deliberate, documented alternation — NOT random section inversion (skill theme-lock). One hot red carries across both grounds identically.

## Type (Doer proposal — confirm on render)
Single strong grotesque super-family for discipline + a mono for data precision. Working pick: **Archivo** (400–900; heavy/tight for display, regular for body — "classic B&W, strong & bold") + **Spline Sans Mono** (data/labels). Both freely licensed (Google Fonts), no invoice. Not on the taste-skill reflex-reject lists. Open to change on the operator's eye.
