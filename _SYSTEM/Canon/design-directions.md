# SBP Website — Design Directions (three candidates)

**Status:** v0.1 — **DRAFT, awaiting the operator's design-direction pick (W1 Step 1).** · **Date:** 2026-06-12 · **Label:** INSTANCE · **Originating record:** `_SYSTEM/Session_Logs/2026-06-12_SBP-W1_SCL.md`
**Binding premise (B-7):** the old system — parchment cream `#f5f1e8` / burnt copper `#b8451f` / Fraunces + Inter Tight + JetBrains Mono, warm editorial spirits-adjacent — is **dead and does not port**. None of the directions below reuse its palette family (warm cream/copper), its typefaces, or its mood. All three are built to sell **software (CRM/Dash) first**, services second, in the locked SBP voice (real, raw, no suits).

**Constants across all three (not up for vote):** mobile-responsive; WCAG-AA contrast; fast static pages; no popups; system respects the compliance set on every page; one accent color used with discipline; real product screenshots over stock anything; all typefaces freely licensed (Google Fonts) — no font invoice.

---

## Direction A — "Control Room"
**Mood:** dark, calm, data-forward. The site looks like the product: a serious operations tool you'd run your business in at 7am. Closest analog: modern dev-tool/analytics sites, but warmed by SBP's human voice rather than cold enterprise.

- **Color:** near-black blue-slate ground `#0D1117`; panel slate `#161B22`; off-white text `#E6EDF3`; **signal green accent `#3FB950`** (data-positive, used for CTAs, live numbers, chart strokes); muted data hues for charts only.
- **Type:** **Space Grotesk** (display — technical but characterful) · **IBM Plex Sans** (body) · **IBM Plex Mono** (numbers, labels, table accents).
- **Texture/imagery:** UI screenshots framed as the hero objects; thin 1px grid lines; subtle terminal/ledger motifs; no photography required at launch.
- **Why it fits:** makes ⟨CRM⟩/⟨Dash⟩ look like world-class software on sight — the B-2 quality bar. The voice supplies the warmth the palette withholds; that contrast (gritty plain talk inside a precise dark UI) is distinctive.
- **Risk:** dark sites can read "crypto/devtool" and feel distant from a brand-owner audience; services pages need care so marketing work doesn't look like an afterthought inside a software shell.

## Direction B — "Shop Floor"
**Mood:** light industrial, documentary, blue-collar honest. Concrete, ink, one safety color. The visual equivalent of "no Madison Avenue suits" — a working shop, not a lounge.

- **Color:** cool paper `#F4F5F3` ground (cool gray-white — deliberately not the old warm cream); ink `#121417`; **safety orange accent `#FF4D00`**; steel gray `#9AA0A6` for secondary.
- **Type:** **Archivo** in heavy/expanded cuts (display — poster-grade, shouts plainly) · **Archivo** regular (body, one family throughout) · **Space Mono** (specs, captions, data callouts).
- **Texture/imagery:** big condensed headlines; ruled sections like a job sheet; documentary photography (real bottles, real shelves, real warehouse) when available, hard-edged screenshot frames meanwhile; visible labels/stamps ("DEVELOPING BRAND — HEAT ON").
- **Why it fits:** the most literal rendering of the SBP voice — raw, earned, anti-agency. Strong differentiation from both agency-pretty and SaaS-slick competitors; products read as tools, which is the truth.
- **Risk:** industrial-brutalist is having a moment in startup-land — needs disciplined art direction to stay "working shop" and not "Figma brutalism template." Orange must be rationed or it cheapens.

## Direction C — "Signal"
**Mood:** light, quiet, precise. White space, one deep accent, weight-contrast typography. Premium-modern without being corporate — confidence so earned it doesn't raise its voice.

- **Color:** white `#FFFFFF` / haze `#F7F8FA` grounds; ink `#16181D`; **deep cobalt accent `#1D4ED8`**; slate `#64748B` secondary.
- **Type:** **Hanken Grotesk** (single family, hard weight contrast: 800 display / 400 body) · **Geist Mono** (numbers and table accents).
- **Texture/imagery:** generous margins; screenshots floated on soft-shadow cards; thin keylines; restrained motion (fade/slide only). Nothing decorative that doesn't inform.
- **Why it fits:** broadest-shouldered option — equally credible holding a CRM page, a services page, and a legal page; ages slowest; easiest to execute consistently fast. The voice carries all the personality against a neutral ground.
- **Risk:** the least *distinct* — done timidly it's every B2B SaaS site of the decade. Lives or dies on typography discipline and copy; the voice has to do real work.

## Recommendation (Doer's, non-binding)
**A ("Control Room") to sell the products hardest; B ("Shop Floor") to embody the brand hardest.** If the next 12 months are mostly *CRM/Dash client acquisition* (the stated driver), A is the sharpest weapon. A hybrid is possible at Step 2 (e.g., B's typographic attitude on A's product-screenshot discipline) but the operator should pick one center of gravity — hybrids drift.

**The gate:** the operator picks one direction (or directs a named hybrid) together with skeleton approval. Mood boards / a sample hero mock per direction can be produced on request before the pick — say the word; nothing builds until the pick.
