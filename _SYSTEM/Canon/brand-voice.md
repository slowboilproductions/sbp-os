## 1. Inherited and locked (not re-opened here)

- **Positioning:** SBP exists for startups and developing brands underserved by the agency model; AI does the heavy lifting; real quality at 50 %+ under market; grow clients until they're too big for us — and celebrate the graduation.
- **The name is the metaphor:** the slow boil is the moment right before the roiling boil. The brand is becoming.
- **Voice character:** authentic, real, raw, gritty; no suits, no Madison Avenue; conversational and direct — a smart friend talking; no marketing fluff; earned confidence.
- **Terminology lock:** **"brand owner"** (never "founder") · **"developing"** (never "emerging").
- **The chef-knife frame** (client-facing): AI as the master chef's sharp knife — not replacing the cook, making every cut cleaner and faster.
- **Closing line (locked):** *"You didn't come this far to fold. Let's get to work."*

## 2. Voice pillars (the new flesh — four, each with an edge)

1. **Earned, not claimed.** We say what we've done and what it costs, in numbers, or we don't say it. *Sounds like:* "Product photography that looks like a $50,000 shoot. It isn't one. That's the point." *Never:* "best-in-class creative solutions."
2. **Operator-to-operator.** Written by someone who has carried inventory, chased a distributor, watched a depletion report land late. Reader is assumed competent and busy. *Sounds like:* "You already know what your distributor's report doesn't tell you." *Never:* explaining the reader's own business to them.
3. **Honest about the machine.** We use AI and say so, plainly — what it does (the heavy lifting) and what it doesn't (the judgment, the taste, the strategy). No mystique, no apology. *Sounds like:* "AI does the heavy lifting. The instincts are still earned." *Never:* "AI-powered" as a magic word, or hiding the AI.
4. **Heat under the surface.** The slow-boil metaphor governs tone: contained intensity, not shouting. Short sentences when it matters. The reader should feel the brand is *about to* boil — momentum, not arrival. *Sounds like:* "Not there yet. But the heat is on." *Never:* exclamation points doing the work the sentence should.

## 3. Lexicon (extends the lock — proposed, operator ratifies)

**Keep saying:** brand owner · developing brand · tools (for CRM/Dash) · "moves product" · "what's actually fair" · graduation.
**Proposed additions to the lock:**
- **"tools you run your business in"** — not "platform," not "solution" (CRM/Dash framing).
- **"what moved"** — the Dash plain-speech for depletions/sell-through on public surfaces (the jargon stays in the product).
- **"we build / we run"** — not "we leverage," "we empower," "we enable."
**Ban list (site-wide):** solutions · synergy · best-in-class · cutting-edge · revolutionize · unlock · empower · seamless · "AI-powered" in headlines · innovative · world-class *(we show the bar, we don't claim the phrase)*.
**Voice-neutral zones:** privacy, terms, and compliance copy are plain and unstyled — voice never costs clarity where law lives.

## 4. Mechanics (how sentences behave)

- Second person, present tense, contractions on. One idea per sentence; cut the second clause first.
- Concrete number > adjective ("50 %+ under market," "five distributor formats, one picture") — every claim on a product page must be true of the real build (verified against unit canon before copy lands).
- Profanity posture: the voice is gritty, the site is clean — "damn"-grade at most on the site, and never in product or legal surfaces. (Raw ≠ crude; proposed, operator's call.)
- Rhythm signature: long setup line, short punch. The locked closing line is the template.

## 5. Voice across the new surfaces (the family flex)

The May site spoke only as the agency. The reset speaks as **one house with two registers**:
- **Products (⟨CRM⟩/⟨Dash⟩):** pillars 1–3 lead — precision, operator empathy, honest machine. The grit shows in *what we choose to say plainly* ("a CRM that knows what a depletion is"), not in attitude.
- **Services (AMS/IC):** the original register — the wall, the fair price, the knife.
- **About/home:** full voice — the origin story is the differentiator and carries the heat.
- **One rule binds all:** the same person is talking on every page. If a product page couldn't have been written by the person who wrote the origin story, it's off-voice.

## 6. Short-form variants (the positioning doc's open list — candidates, illustrative, NOT locked copy)

These discharge the "Short-Form Variants (To Be Developed)" list the locked doc carries. They are voice *proposals* for the operator to react to, not drafted site copy (the no-content-before-gate rule holds; these exist to make the voice judgeable):

- **Headline (≤10 words):** "Real marketing. Real tools. Prices that don't insult you." / "The heat is on." / "Big-brand firepower. Developing-brand prices."
- **One-sentence answer:** "Slow Boil builds the marketing and the tools — CRM, distribution analytics, creative — that developing brands need to compete, at prices that don't require a fortune."
- **Elevator (30s, spoken):** the origin story's paras 5–7 compressed: the wall → AI as the knife → fair price → graduation.
- **Boilerplate (2 sentences):** "Slow Boil Productions builds marketing and business tools for developing consumer brands — strategy, creative, a CPG-native CRM, and distribution analytics — using AI to do the heavy lifting so the price stays fair. We grow brands until they're too big for us, and we celebrate that."
